How to read this page

  1. The questions. On a task page, scroll to "Question tree". In any view, they are in the "Question tree" window on the right. Click it to open the full tree.
  2. The answer tree. Each box is one yes/no question with its answer, confidence and where the evidence was found. The solid line going down is the path this run took. A dashed arrow to the right shows what would have happened with the other answer.
  3. The final answer. The box at the bottom of the tree. Each view also shows it in its own style.
  4. Cost, time and confidence. At the top of the question tree window: questions asked, cost in dollars, seconds and confidence. Tokens are the small number under them.
  5. Your review. Keep, Skip or Hurts on any question, plus a note. The bar above the tree counts what you flagged.

Everything here is simulated demo data. Confidence is what the model reports, not checked accuracy.
